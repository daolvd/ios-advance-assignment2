====task====
Decide which listed options the customer asked for, for one product they ordered.
====end-task====

====rules====
Only choose options from the allowed list. Nothing else is available for this product.
Choose an option only if the customer asked for it about this product.
If they asked for no changes, return one group with all the units and no options.

Split the units into groups when the customer wants different options on different
units. "Two burgers, one without mayonnaise" is one group of 1 with that option and
one group of 1 without it.
The group quantities must add up to the total ordered.
====end-rules====
